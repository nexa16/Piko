Principles to agree on

  1. Quiet, not faint. Readable contrast, less decorative copy.
  2. Task first. Activity above collections; Settings top-right; one route to Statistics.
  3. One visual language. One icon family, consistent spacing, restrained radii.
  4. Color has a job. Brand accent stays distinct from success and error.
  5. Readable at real sizes. Start at 16px body text with comfortable 44–48px touch targets.
  6. Design evolves together. Accepted decisions update copy, tokens, specification, and mockup together.

Proposed principles. The preview starts with the accepted Minimum flow. Switch to Expanded to check how additional actions fit.
