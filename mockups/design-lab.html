Design rules · UX acceptance criteria

Open design decisions

  • Review exact reading-mascot placement/scale before integration. Later Speed-completion feedback needs placement/dismissal/lifetime design while retaining direct return, no new summary popup and no second Collection mascot.
  • Research recommends consistent icon-only Back controls nearer the safe-area edges, preserving a 48dp Android target and localized accessible names. Global adoption and exact shared geometry await owner review; review-screen icon-only Back is already accepted.
  • Tablet and desktop layouts beyond removing duplicate navigation are not yet defined.
  • Dark theme is not selected or included in the agreed round-1 settings.
