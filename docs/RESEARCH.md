# Research plan

## Research goals

Validate the user problem, identify the first target user, understand their current approach, and find the smallest useful outcome. Determine relevant privacy and data constraints before implementation.

## Target participant description

To be decided: Who experiences the proposed problem, in what context, and what inclusion criteria should guide recruitment? Do not recruit against an invented user profile.

## Proposed interview questions

- What task or outcome are you trying to accomplish?
- Tell us about the last time you tried to do it.
- What steps did you take, and where did you encounter difficulty?
- What do you use today, and what works or does not work?
- How often does this situation occur, and what is its impact?
- What would a useful outcome look like?
- What information would you be comfortable providing, and what should remain private?
- What constraints would prevent you from using a new approach?

Adapt these questions after the user problem is defined. Avoid leading participants toward an assumed solution.

## Public repository privacy warning

**Do not commit names, contact details, recordings, transcripts, credentials, health data, or other identifying information.** Keep raw research in a separate private location with appropriate consent and access controls. Anonymize findings and remove combinations of details that could identify a participant before publishing. Do not include links to private records or a participant lookup key.

## Anonymized findings template

- **Research question:** To be decided — what was investigated?
- **Method and period:** To be decided — how and when was evidence gathered?
- **Participant group:** To be decided — what broad, non-identifying criteria describe the group?
- **Observation:** To be decided — what happened, summarized without identifying details?
- **Evidence strength:** To be decided — how many independent observations support it?
- **Interpretation:** To be decided — what might the observation mean?
- **Limitations:** To be decided — what uncertainty or bias remains?
- **Product implication:** To be decided — what proposed change follows?
- **Follow-up question:** To be decided — what still needs evidence?

## Converting findings into planning changes

1. Review consent and remove identifying details before adding a finding here.
2. Distinguish observations from interpretations and assumptions; document evidence strength and limitations.
3. Propose the relevant change to PRODUCT.md, with a link to the anonymized finding.
4. Obtain the owner's decision when the finding materially changes the product definition or scope.
5. Record accepted choices in DECISIONS.md and update PRODUCT.md and ROADMAP.md together.
6. Leave unresolved implications as explicit questions rather than treating them as requirements.
